01 · Overview

Reservoir simulation examples in PhysicsNeMo-Sym

A family of open-source examples, authored end to end, that show how physics-informed neural operators replace the forward simulator in reservoir engineering and carbon storage, from training through to Bayesian history matching.

2D and 3D black-oil

Forward problems with FNO, PINO and AFNO surrogates, trained with data and the PDE residual.

Norne

Forward and inverse problems on the Norne field model.

CCUS

CO2-brine flow for carbon capture, utilisation and storage, with a physics-constrained neural operator.

Generative workflows

Latent priors from variational autoencoders and diffusion models for history matching.

Numerical solvers

A black-oil simulator, a CO2 convective-mixing simulator and a history-matching solver.

02 · Carbon storage

CO2-brine flow with a physics-constrained neural operator

Geological storage of CO2 needs forecasts of the plume and the pressure over hundreds of years, for many realisations of the subsurface. The CCUS example couples a CO2-brine thermodynamic model (phase split by flash, phase densities and viscosities) with a physics-constrained neural operator whose loss includes the discretised mass-conservation residuals of both components. The physics-informed operator keeps its accuracy over the full storage period where a data-only operator drifts, at a fraction of the simulator's cost.

05 · Live demo

Ensemble history matching in the browser

Ensemble history matching in miniature. Two unknowns, near-well permeability and inter-well connectivity, control when water breaks through at two producers. Choose alpha-REKI, the adaptive regularised ensemble Kalman inversion, which picks each inflation alpha from the data misfit and stops when the sum of 1/alpha reaches one, or ES-MDA with a fixed number of assimilations. Watch the ensemble collapse onto the hidden truth and the predictions onto the data.

A toy forward model of breakthrough curves stands in for the neural operator; the update is the full ensemble Kalman step with the ensemble covariances and perturbed observations.
